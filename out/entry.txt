Trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi between Ukraine, Russia, and the United States concluded with an agreement for a major prisoner exchange, though negotiators reached no ceasefire or territorial settlement. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy called the sessions substantive, but NATO scrambled jets following drone incursions into Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia. A US Army officer was arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying for Moscow.

In the Strait of Hormuz, closed for over 250 days, Iran’s security chief said the waterway will not reopen until the US meets seven conditions for peace. Iranian officials warned the strait's status has permanently shifted as naval mines damaged three tankers Tuesday. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said economic pressure remains the primary strategy, but the US may respond to further provocations. The DOJ charged three Iran-backed operatives over West-based plots.

The US House effectively ended the impeachment of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth by referring the resolution to committee. In Antalya, the COP31 summit opened with UN Secretary-General Guterres warning of global destruction without immediate climate action. Five nations pledged to double rooftop solar capacity by 2030.

Israeli strikes in Gaza killed at least seven people as regional truces frayed. Israel’s new parliament was sworn in amid political deadlock, leaving Prime Minister Netanyahu without a majority. In DR Congo, officials said a Bundibugyo-strain Ebola outbreak is now the second deadliest on record. Conflicts intensified in Yemen and Sudan, with heavy Houthi casualties reported in government strikes. President Trump says he expects to meet Xi Jinping and possibly Vladimir Putin at next week's APEC summit in Shenzhen.

Talks in Abu Dhabi involving Ukraine, Russia, and the United States concluded with an agreement for a large-scale prisoner exchange, though negotiators failed to reach a ceasefire or territorial settlement. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy described the initial sessions as substantive, but regional tensions escalated as NATO scrambled jets following drone incursions into Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes. Meanwhile, a US Army officer was arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying for Russia.

In the Strait of Hormuz, which has remained closed for over 250 days, Iran’s security chief stated the waterway will not reopen until the United States meets seven specific conditions for peace. Iranian officials warned the strait's status has permanently shifted, while naval mines reportedly damaged three tankers on Tuesday. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio indicated that while economic pressure remains the primary strategy, the United States is prepared to respond to further provocations. Separately, the US Department of Justice charged three Iran-backed operatives over multiple plots in the West.

In Washington, the House of Representatives effectively ended the impeachment effort against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth by referring the resolution to the Judiciary Committee. In Antalya, the COP31 climate summit opened with UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres warning of global destruction without immediate action. Five nations endorsed a declaration to double rooftop solar capacity by 2030.

Israeli strikes in Gaza killed at least seven people as regional truces showed signs of fraying. Israel’s new parliament was sworn in amid political deadlock, leaving Prime Minister Netanyahu without a majority. In the Democratic Republic of Congo, officials reported that a Bundibugyo-strain Ebola outbreak has become the second deadliest on record. Conflicts also intensified in Yemen and Sudan, with heavy casualties reported in government strikes against Houthi positions. Looking ahead, President Trump expects to meet with Xi Jinping and potentially Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit in Shenzhen next week.
